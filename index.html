How it started

Everything began with planning in November 2024. Here is the path from that start to where we are heading.

  1. November 2024

    Planning begins. The idea for Zwide Global Holdings takes shape as one company covering six industries.

  2. 2025

    Groundwork. Planning continues across logistics, mining, drilling, agriculture, construction and architecture.

  3. 2026Now

    Architecture opens. We are taking architecture jobs and enquiries, and admissions are open for the divisions still to come.

  4. Late 2027

    The remaining divisions launch: logistics, mining, drilling, agriculture and construction.
